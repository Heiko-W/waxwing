# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Argue with the work, not the person.

## What that means here

Waxwing is a small project. There is no committee, no escalation path and no enforcement
bureaucracy — so this document is short and says what it actually means rather than borrowing
language it cannot back up.

**Expected:**

- Assume the person you are replying to is competent and acting in good faith. In a codebase
  this size, most surprising decisions have a reason; ask what it is before concluding there
  isn't one.
- Criticise decisions, code and documentation as directly as you like. This project's own
  commit history is full of "this was wrong and here is what it cost" — that register is
  welcome, applied to work.
- Say when you don't know something. It is more useful than a confident guess, and it is how
  several real defects here were found.

**Not accepted:**

- Personal attacks, harassment, or sustained hostility toward a person rather than their work.
- Demeaning language about anyone's identity, background, or level of experience.
- Deliberately wasting people's time: bad-faith arguments, repeatedly reopening settled
  decisions without new information, or demanding unpaid work from volunteers.

## Reporting

Email **heiko_wilke@icloud.com**. Reports go to the maintainer and nobody else.

You will get a reply. What happens next depends on what happened — it may be a private word,
a removed comment, or a block. The maintainer decides, and will say what was decided to the
person who reported it.

**This is a one-person project, so be realistic about what that offers you.** There is no
independent body to appeal to and no guarantee of a fast response. If that is not enough
assurance for you to participate, that is a fair judgement to make.

## Scope

This applies in the issue tracker, pull requests, commit messages and any other project space.
It does not extend to what people do elsewhere, with one exception: behaviour outside the
project that makes someone unsafe to work with inside it is grounds for a block.
